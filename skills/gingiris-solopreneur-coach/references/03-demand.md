# Validate demand and fit / 需求验证与客户适配

## English

Use when choosing whom to approach or deciding whether a request can become an order. Draft outreach; sending requires authorization. Prefer relevant relationships, permitted community conversations, or consent-based introductions. No response is unknown, not proof of no demand; respect refusals and contact preferences.

Invitation draft: “You mentioned [specific task]. Is it still something you are working on? I am exploring [bounded help]. If relevant, I can share a short scope and we can check whether it fits.” Do not imply a prior relationship that does not exist.

Ask only what changes the decision, across the conversation rather than as a questionnaire:
- What happened recently that made the task matter now?
- What artifact or current workflow shows the problem? What has already been tried?
- Who will use the result, for what decision or action?
- Who approves scope and spending, and who handles payment?
- What deadline, client inputs, and implementation capacity exist?

Separate original statements, artifacts, and your proposed solution. Check task evidence, capability fit, use/execution, purchasing process, aligned expectations, and your capacity. A title, funding amount, or friendly response is not enough.

Choose: quote a clear task; propose a separately useful paid diagnosis; bounded pilot; wait for a missing condition; or decline/refer. A paid diagnosis must have standalone value, not merely rename a sales proposal.

Write a short recap: problem / evidence and unknowns / proposed scope / user of output / client responsibilities / exclusions / acceptance / next owner and checkpoint. Invite correction before turning it into a firm quote.

If the client wants broad growth but only has a designer available, a page-message audit may fit; unconfirmed development or guaranteed revenue does not. If scope keeps changing, settle one current task before quoting implementation.

Start with one relevant invitation. Set response timing to the context; follow up only when appropriate and authorized, stop on refusal, and keep silence as unknown. Qualitative conversations guide the next test, not statistical proof of a market.

## 中文

适用于选择联系对象，或判断请求是否适合接单。起草邀约；实际发送需授权。优先相关关系、允许交流的社区或经同意的转介绍。未回复记未知，不代表没有需求；尊重拒绝和联系偏好。

邀约草稿：“看到你提到〔具体任务〕，想确认是否还在推进。我正在探索〔有边界的帮助〕。如果相关，可以先发一份简短范围说明，一起判断是否匹配。”不虚构关系。

只问影响判断的问题，随对话逐步了解，不一次发问卷：
- 最近发生什么，让任务现在变得重要？
- 什么材料或现有流程能说明问题？已尝试什么？
- 谁使用成果，用于什么决定或行动？
- 谁确认范围和预算，谁处理付款？
- 截止日期、客户输入与实施容量是什么？

分开原话、材料与拟议方案。检查任务证据、能力适配、使用与执行、采购流程、预期和自身容量。职位、融资或友好回应都不足以判断。

决定进入明确任务报价、独立有用的付费诊断、有边界试合作、等待条件，或拒绝/转介。付费诊断必须有独立价值，不能只是销售提案改名。

写短复述：问题、证据与未知、建议范围、成果使用人、客户责任、排除项、验收、下一负责人和检查点。允许纠正后再形成正式报价。

客户要求全面增长但只有设计师可配合时，可考虑页面表达诊断；未确认开发与保证营收不适合承诺。范围不断变化时，先固定一个当前任务再报实施价。

从一段相关邀约开始。回应窗口按实际情境设置，仅在适当且获授权时跟进，拒绝后停止，沉默记未知。定性沟通用于决定下一实验，不证明整个市场。
