# Deliver and review / 交付与复盘

## English

Use the confirmed scope and latest agreed version. Check start conditions, materials, responsible people, acceptance criteria, and payment state. Separate advisory judgment from implementation; identify who executes.

1. Plan backward from agreed outputs and dependencies. Set a checkpoint proportional to duration and uncertainty, not mandatory meetings for every tiny job.
2. Review evidence, assumptions, completeness, formats, links, access, and actual claims. Include AI-output review in the workload. Do not report business impact merely because a report was delivered.
3. Submit outputs with scope mapping, limitations, client actions, and the agreed feedback window. Classify feedback as a missed commitment, clarification, or new scope. Fix original errors without charging for a new feature.
4. For changes, describe the request, original version, cost/time/dependency impact, new acceptance, and mutual confirmation before execution. If materials are late, explain impact and agree a revised plan.
5. Obtain recorded acceptance; silence is not acceptance without an applicable agreed basis. Track outstanding invoices or purchasing steps until actual settlement. Keep support/handover obligations visible after payment.

For recurring advisory, define the cycle goal, client execution owner, meeting cadence, asynchronous support hours/channel, and renewal checkpoint. Keep one rolling record: previous action/owner → evidence → blocker → decision → next action/owner/date. Review last actions before adding ideas. Renew only when there is a concrete need, mutual value, and capacity; do not default to indefinite support.

Review all time, cash received/outstanding/refunded, direct costs, scope changes, rework, actual client use, and desired work style. Choose repeat, narrow, reprice, improve a reusable asset, or stop. If results are unknown, say so. Ask permission before sharing any case, client identity, image, or quote.

First complete paid loop requires delivered scope, recorded acceptance, all due agreed payments settled, clear remaining obligations, and a review. Subsequent market demand and repeatability still need evidence. An optional later check asks whether the client used the result; no reply remains unknown.

15-minute first action: compare one deliverable with its acceptance criterion and identify the missing evidence or owner.

## 中文

使用最新确认的范围与版本。核对启动条件、材料、负责人、验收标准与付款状态。区分顾问判断和代执行，明确谁实施。

1. 从约定成果及依赖倒排计划，按周期和未知设置检查点，不要求每个小任务都开会。
2. 核查证据、假设、完整性、格式、链接、权限和效果表述。AI 输出复核计入工作量；交报告不代表业务效果发生。
3. 提交时说明成果与范围对应、限制、客户动作和约定反馈窗口。区分原承诺遗漏、澄清、新增范围；原交付错误不借新增功能名义收费。
4. 变更写清请求、原版本、费用时间依赖影响、新验收及双方确认，再执行。材料迟到时说明影响，协商新计划。
5. 取得有记录的验收；没有适用约定依据时，沉默不能视为通过。跟踪发票和采购步骤直到实际到账，收款后仍保留支持与移交义务。

周期陪跑明确本周期目标、客户执行人、会议节奏、异步渠道与支持时间、续约检查点。维护一份连续记录：上次动作/负责人→证据→阻塞→决定→下次动作/负责人/日期。先检查旧动作再增加想法。有具体需求、双方价值和容量才续约，不默认无限支持。

复盘全部工时、到账/待收/退款、直接支出、范围变化、返工、客户实际使用和期望工作方式。决定重复、缩小、改价、改善复用材料或停止。结果未知就注明；公开案例、身份、图片、引语前取得授权。

完整首单闭环需要约定交付完成、验收有记录、应付约定款项结清、剩余义务明确、完成复盘。市场重复需求仍需证据。可选后续回访询问成果是否使用，未回复仍记未知。

15分钟起手动作：对照一个交付物与验收标准，找出缺少的证据或负责人。
