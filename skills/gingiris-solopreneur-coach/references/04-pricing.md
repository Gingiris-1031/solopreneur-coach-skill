# Scope and price / 范围与报价

## English

Start with a defined task, output use, acceptance method, dependencies, and capacity. If these are unclear, return to demand. Choose one format that fits: consultation, fixed project, recurring advisory, standalone diagnosis, or workshop. Do not force three packages.

Estimate all work: sales, preparation, production/meetings, quality review, revisions, communication, closing, and promised support. Add direct expenses and identify excluded overhead/tax costs. An internal calculation (hours × chosen planning rate + expenses) checks feasibility; it does not establish market price or net profit.

Draft a quote with: version/date, goal, confirmed scope, outputs/quantity, client inputs, communication/revisions/support, exclusions/dependencies, acceptance person/method, start conditions and timeline, price/currency/tax treatment, payment milestones, change process, validity, and next checkpoint. Mark missing commercial details instead of inventing them.

Select payment arrangements from actual work, cash exposure, and buyer process; no universal deposit ratio or legal clause. If post-payment is agreed, label it as such. Confirm account/currency/reconciliation before giving payment instructions. No legal or tax conclusions without appropriate current sources and context.

If price is rejected, clarify which result matters most and whether scope, timing, trust, budget, or procurement is blocking progress. Change one major condition at a time. Narrow the deliverable instead of silently offering unlimited work at a discount. A discount needs a reason, boundaries, and expiry. Do not retroactively change an existing agreement.

Record: no response / scope unresolved / budget issue / procurement pending / rejected / scope and price confirmed / payment received. A signed agreement is not cash; a deposit is not completion. If results-based pricing is requested, clarify control, measurement, attribution, and exclusions before agreeing.

15-minute first action: add three easily missed activities to the estimate. After delivery, compare expected and actual hours, scope changes, and recorded expenses before changing price. Cash received minus recorded direct costs per total hour is a limited comparison, not net profit.

## 中文

先确认任务、成果用途、验收、依赖与容量；不清楚就返回需求阶段。选择适合的一种形式：单次咨询、固定项目、周期陪跑、独立诊断或工作坊，不强制三档套餐。

估计全部工作：售前、准备、制作/会议、核查、修改、沟通、结项、承诺支持。加入直接支出，标出未计经营成本与税费。内部测算“工时×自选测算值＋支出”用于判断负担，不等于市场售价或净利润。

报价包含：版本日期、目标、已确认范围、成果数量、客户输入、沟通修改支持、排除项与依赖、验收人及方法、启动条件与周期、金额币种税费口径、付款节点、变更流程、有效期和下一检查点。缺少的商务信息标待确认，不编造。

按实际工作、现金支出与采购流程选择付款方式，不套固定首款比例或法律条款。约定后付就如实记录。提供付款指引前确认账号、币种与对账条件；没有适用的当前来源和背景，不下法律税务结论。

价格被拒时，了解最重要成果以及范围、时间、信任、预算或采购中的阻塞，每次改变一个主要条件。优先缩成果，不暗中用无限工作换折扣。折扣明确原因、范围和期限，不追溯单方面修改既有约定。

记录未回复、范围待定、预算问题、采购等待、拒绝、范围价格确认、款项到账。签约不等于到账，首款不等于完成。要求按结果收费时，先明确控制范围、测量、归因和排除项。

15分钟起手动作：补上三项容易漏算的工作。交付后比较估计与实际工时、范围变化和已记录支出，再决定改价。“到账减已记录直接支出，再除全部小时”仅供有限比较，不是净利润。
