# Method notes / 方法说明

## English

This draft adapts user-supplied interviews with Iris, the Gingiris OPC starting guide, and service-productization SOPs developed in this task. The operational references are edited, bilingual instructions rather than verbatim transcript translations. No private transcripts, client records, or historical price claims are included.

Related source: Gingiris OPC starting and service-productization guide (user-supplied source). Access may require permission; reading it is not a runtime dependency. Related public capabilities: [Gingiris Skills](https://tools.gingiris.com/skills/).

Contribution/evidence separation, bounded offers, buyer research, scope, acceptance, settlement, and review inform the workflow. Suggested timeboxes are adjustable design choices. Simulated examples are fictional; neither structural validation nor simulated conversations demonstrate market demand or income outcomes.
